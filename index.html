D3COMPOSURE is an independent clothing and art label based in San Francisco.

We release small, numbered pieces — D3 01, D3 02, D3 03 — each built around an original hand-drawn artwork, alongside work on canvas.

Sustainability

How each piece is made

01 · Made to order

Nothing is produced until it is ordered. There is no seasonal overstock and no warehouse of unsold garments to discard at the end of a season.

02 · Material

100% cotton, sourced locally by our manufacturer.

03 · Made in Portugal

Every garment is made in Portugal, where textile production is held to EU labour and environmental law. Short supply chains mean fewer transport miles between fabric and finished piece.

04 · Made to last

Machine wash cold, inside out, and hang dry. Caring for a garment this way keeps the print sharp and the fabric in use for longer, which is the most sustainable thing any piece of clothing can do.
