About

Founder's note

D3COMPOSURE was founded in 2000.

We make small, limited collections and restock each season.

Each piece brings abstract art into everyday clothing, using archival fabrics, hand-drawn prints and work on canvas.

Everything is made to order in Portugal, which keeps waste low and moves us toward a more sustainable future.

01 Occupational health & safety

ISO 45001

ISO 45001 is the international standard for occupational health and safety. It helps protect employees and visitors from work-related injuries and illnesses. The standard builds on earlier systems like OHSAS 18001 and follows ILO labor and safety guidelines.
