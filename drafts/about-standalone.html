D3COMPOSURE

Founder’s note

d3composure was founded in 2000. We restock inventory seasonally and invite you to explore our collection. Our collections are carefully selected and limited, exploring ideas in the form of archival fabrics and various mediums on canvas. We represent a movement towards a sustainable future and in integrating the art in the abstract and uncensored ideology in our everyday fashion.

What we make

Outerwear and knitwear cut in small runs, alongside original work on canvas. Each piece begins with the fabric — archival stock, sourced in limited quantity — which means a garment exists only as long as the cloth does.

How we release

Collections drop seasonally and are not reproduced. When a piece sells out it stays sold out. Anything still listed on the site is still available.

Materials and making

Working from archival and deadstock cloth keeps existing material in use rather than commissioning new production. Garments are finished in small batches.
